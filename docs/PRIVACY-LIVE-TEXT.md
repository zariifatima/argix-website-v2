# Original privacy-policy paragraphs from argix.net (24 September 2026)

The new site's policy was edited on 7 October 2026 to match what the new site actually does (no form service, no analytics).
If you later connect a form service or Google Analytics, update `src/content/privacy.json` to describe it. The paragraphs
below are the wording used on the live site for those two features, kept here for reference. Have the final text reviewed
by whoever is responsible for Argix's legal and privacy documents.

**Form service (live site):** Messages sent through the contact form are delivered to us by a form-handling service, which processes them only to pass them to our inbox.

**Analytics (live site):** With your consent, we use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use this site, for example which pages are viewed and how people found us. We also count when someone sends the contact form or clicks our WhatsApp or email links, without recording what they write. Google Analytics sets cookies (named `_ga` and `_ga_` followed by an ID) that last up to two years. Analytics cookies are only set if you accept. Before you choose, or if you decline, Google Analytics sets no cookies and receives only limited information about the visit, such as the page viewed and the type of browser, which Google uses to estimate overall usage.
